From scrapped pack to second life

Four steps, each one checked and recorded so every product can be traced back to its source pack.

Step 1

Collect

We pick up retired packs from EV owners, fleets and service centres.

Step 2

Test and grade

Every cell is measured for capacity and internal resistance. Weak cells are removed.

Step 3

Rebuild

Matched cells are assembled with a new battery management system and safe casing.

Step 4

Certify and ship

Each unit passes a full charge cycle and safety test before dispatch.

Good for riders, better for the planet

An EV battery is usually retired with most of its life left. We put that capacity back to work instead of sending it to landfill.

Lower cost

Second-life packs cost well below new lithium batteries.

Less waste

Cells that can’t be reused go to certified material recovery.

Safe by design

Over-charge, over-heat and short-circuit protection in every unit.
